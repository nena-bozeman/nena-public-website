The **Bozeman Local Government Study Commission** has been working over the past year and a half to learn more about the structure of our city government — known as a **City Charter** — and the draft report and recommendations are ready for review.

We're seeking your thoughts and recommendations on **Thursday, June 18** starting at **5 p.m.** (with food!) followed by public comment **5:30–7:30 p.m.** in a public hearing at **City Hall**. These recommended amendments to our city charter are preliminary; what is decided in July will go to voters on the **November 3** ballot.

## How to participate

- Review the [draft amended Charter and Study Commission 2024–26 Report](http://www.bozemanstudy.com) on the commission website
- Join us in person at City Hall (pizza and more!) or watch online starting at 5:00 p.m. — you can share your thoughts in person and online
- Submit written comments and suggestions by **noon on June 18** to [govreview@bozemanmt.gov](mailto:govreview@bozemanmt.gov)
- Share this with friends, family, and networks

Every voice matters. NENA neighbor **Barb Cestero** serves on the Bozeman Local Government Study Commission.

See the [event listing](/events/2026-06-18-bozeman-draft-city-charter-public-hearing) for time and location details.
