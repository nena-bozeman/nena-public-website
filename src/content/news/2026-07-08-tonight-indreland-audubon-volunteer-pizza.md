Same-day reminder for **[NENA Wetland Volunteer Night](/events/2026-07-08-nena-wetland-volunteer-night)** at the [Indreland Audubon Wetland Preserve](/places/indreland-audubon-wetland-preserve): volunteer with the Audubon Society, then pizza and refreshments.

Please [RSVP on Partiful](https://partiful.com/e/tAJPE1fOnFRdtq5gUq8w) so organizers can order enough pizza.

## Parking

1. **Village Downtown Blvd** (recommended) — near the mailboxes just east of N. Broadway Ave; follow the trail sign to the gate (~0.2 mile).
2. **Village Crossing Way** — gravel trail at the southeast end to the gate; do **not** use the private Kestrel Apartments lot.
3. **Behind DA Davidson, 1101 E. Main St.** — northeast corner of the lot; dirt trail downhill to the gate.

See also the [June 30 invite](/news/2026-06-30-indreland-audubon-work-party-7-8-5-8pm).
