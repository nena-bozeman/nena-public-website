The **NENA Spring Meeting** was held **Monday, April 13, 2026**, at the **Public Safety Center**, **Community Room** (**901 N. Rouse**), following the **5:30 p.m.** potluck and **6:00 p.m.** meeting start.

The published agenda included:

- Welcome and **elections** for President and Vice President  
- **Treasurer** report  
- **Landmark Program** update (City Commissioner Alison Sweeney)  
- Inter-Neighborhood Council **interim zoning** request (NENA INC rep Angie Kociolek)  
- NE business spotlight: **Black Petal Farms**  
- **Water Conservation Program** (Jessica Ahlstrom, City of Bozeman)  
- **Visioning Bozeman Creek** (Cathy Costakis)  
- **Pizza + Story Night** recap and next steps (Suzanne Held and Courtney Scott)  
- Announcements
