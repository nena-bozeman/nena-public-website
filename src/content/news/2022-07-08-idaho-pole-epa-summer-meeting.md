Roger Hoogerheide ([Hoogerheide.Roger@epa.gov](mailto:Hoogerheide.Roger@epa.gov)) was scheduled to present at the Summer Meeting. NENA asked Roger to address:

- A brief summary and update on the potential for airborne contaminants, including dioxins, and what the plan is for mitigating the risks associated with them
- How the neighborhood will stay informed as the EPA moves forward with the remediation
- Whether the EPA will stay involved if remediation takes place and then development takes place
- Whether the EPA will still be involved when the City of Bozeman makes decisions such as stormwater drainage
- The in-situ amendments presented in the fact sheet

See the fact sheet here: [2022 05-19 Fact Sheet_508.pdf]({{ url:site }}files/download/ff06ed062887086)
