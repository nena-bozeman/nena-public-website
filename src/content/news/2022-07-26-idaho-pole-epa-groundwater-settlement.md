The EPA signed the Administrative Settlement Agreement and Order on Consent with Idaho Pole Company for groundwater work. EPA anticipates groundwater amendments will begin on August 8 as a pilot in support of the Focused Feasibility Study. The document is available on the EPA website under Site Documents and Data. EPA will update the Announcements and Key Topics section as soon as possible to reflect document availability.

The direct address to the Administrative Order is: [semspub.epa.gov/src/document/08/100011921](https://semspub.epa.gov/src/document/08/100011921)

EPA's Idaho Pole webpage: [www.epa.gov/superfund/idaho-pole](http://www.epa.gov/superfund/idaho-pole)
