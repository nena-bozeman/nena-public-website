Mark your calendar for the Northeast Neighborhood Association (NENA) annual Parade of Sheds on **Sunday, October 18** from **11am-4pm**! This event celebrates the community with a fun, down-to-earth tour of local sheds, yards, murals, homes, and alleyways.

### Schedule

* **11am** - Parade starts at Tinworks Art (**[719 N. Ida Ave](https://www.google.com/maps/search/719+N.+Ida+Ave?entry=gmail&source=g)**) with a casual stroll, costumes, and decorated bikes for all ages.
* **12-1:30pm** - Potluck cookout at Tinworks Art. Drop off items needing refrigeration prior to the parade.
* **2-4pm** - Self-guided tour of open neighbor locations using a provided map.

For questions, contact NENA at [nenabozeman@gmail.com](mailto:nenabozeman@gmail.com?subject=2025%20Parade%20of%20Sheds!). You can also [Donate $5 or More to get your NENA "Last Best Neighborhood" Sticker](https://www.nenabozeman.org/donate "Donate $5 or More to get your NENA \"Last Best Neighborhood\" Sticker").

Stay connected with NENA through their [Website](https://www.nenabozeman.org/), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nenabozeman/), and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/BozemanNENA/).
