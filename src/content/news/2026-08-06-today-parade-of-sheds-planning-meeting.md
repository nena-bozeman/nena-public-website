Same-day reminder for the **[Parade of Sheds planning meeting](/events/2026-08-06-parade-of-sheds-planning-meeting)** — a fun way to be part of the neighborhood.

Join **today, Thursday, August 6, 4:30–6:00 p.m. at Hope and Matt’s house, 702 E. Peach**. Everyone is welcome — you don’t need to have helped organize before or been to the event. We’ll share what we’ve done in the past and make a plan for this year.

This year’s [Parade of Sheds](/our-work/parade-of-sheds) is **[Sunday, October 18](/events/2026-10-18-parade-of-sheds)**. You can play a role by joining the organizing team.

Questions? Email [nenabozeman@gmail.com](mailto:nenabozeman@gmail.com).

See also the [August 3 invite](/news/2026-08-03-parade-of-sheds-planning-meeting).
