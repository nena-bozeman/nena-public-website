NENA looked forward to meeting and hearing from the potential developer at the NENA Meeting on July 21. He was scheduled to briefly present where things stand, talk about the vision for the space, and address questions and concerns.

Two stewards of the neighborhood recently submitted a public comment on the Proposed Purchaser Agreement between EPA and Scout DAC LLC regarding the Idaho Pole Company Superfund Site in Bozeman, MT.
