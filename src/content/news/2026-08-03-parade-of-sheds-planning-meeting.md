Help NENA make this year’s [Parade of Sheds](/our-work/parade-of-sheds) the best ever.

Join the planning meeting **Thursday, August 6, 4:30–6:00 p.m. at 702 E. Peach**. Everyone is welcome — you don’t need to have organized before or been to the event. We’ll share what we’ve done in the past and make a plan for this year.

This year’s parade will be held on **[Sunday, October 18, 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.](/events/2026-10-18-parade-of-sheds)**. You can play a role by joining the organizing team.
