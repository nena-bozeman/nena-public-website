The Northeast Neighborhood has some of Bozeman’s clearest built examples of **high-density to low-density transitions**. NENA invited neighbors to join a **Walk with a Commissioner** to see what those transitions look like, learn more about the current code, and offer input on solutions that work for everyone — including how the Commission can mitigate impacts of high-density development on existing residents.

**All are welcome** — NENA residents, people who live in built areas west of 19th with significant not-yet-built zone density transitions, and anyone curious about transitions.

**Meet at [Beall Park](/places/beall-park), near the pavilion, at 6:00 p.m.**

- [Wednesday, July 22](/events/2026-07-22-walk-with-a-commissioner-density-transitions)
- [Monday, July 27](/events/2026-07-27-walk-with-a-commissioner-density-transitions)

The tour walks about eight blocks outbound, with about the same distance back to the starting point.

More info on the [City of Bozeman calendar](https://www.bozemanmt.gov/Home/Components/Calendar/Event/37675/3126).
