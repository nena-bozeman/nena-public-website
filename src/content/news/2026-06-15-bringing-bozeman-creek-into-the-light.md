The **Bozeman Daily Chronicle** published a Sunday feature on how Bozeman Creek runs largely underground through downtown — and on the city's work with **See Bozeman Creek** and consultants to daylight the creek, improve public access, and reduce flood risk over the coming decades.

- Much of the creek downtown flows through tunnels and culverts, including a 120-year-old tunnel beneath Main Street that engineers say is a flood bottleneck.
- A recent engineering study found downtown infrastructure is not built for a 100-year flood; fixing it now is cheaper than waiting.
- **PORT** presented draft ideas at a June 2 community event; more public input is planned around Sweet Pea in August, with a fuller plan this fall and priority projects to the city commission in December.
