Neighbors are invited to drop in at an Open House to see how community input has shaped the Draft Bozeman Creek Vision Plan. 

**Monday, November 9, 2026**
**4:30–7:30 pm**
**Bozeman City Hall**

The City, [SeeBozemanCreek](https://seebozemancreek.org/), and the design team will share details on flood mitigation strategies, new downtown creekside parks, better connectivity, and future policy ideas. 

For more information, visit [engage.bozemanmt.gov/bzncreek](https://engage.bozemanmt.gov/bzncreek). If you have questions, comments, or ideas, you can reach out to [NENABozeman@gmail.com](mailto:NENABozeman@gmail.com).
