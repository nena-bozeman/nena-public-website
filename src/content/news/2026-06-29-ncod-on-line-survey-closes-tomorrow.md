Reminder that the City’s **NCOD Engagement Survey** was open through **June 30, 2026**, as Bozeman updates design standards and guidelines within the **Neighborhood Conservation Overlay District (NCOD)**.

Neighbors were encouraged to take the survey whether or not they attended the April public events. The survey is fairly long and detailed — respondents could jump ahead to visual-preference questions near the end, and needed to click **Done** for responses to be recorded.

For project background and materials (including Community Open House slides), see:
