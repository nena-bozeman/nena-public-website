Work continues on the project to update the design standards and guidelines that apply within the **Neighborhood Conservation Overlay District (NCOD)**. The project team has been reviewing feedback received in April through the community open house, neighborhood tours, stakeholder listening sessions, and meetings with a neighborhood focus group and the Historic Preservation Advisory Board (HPAB).

## Take the NCOD Engagement Survey

A survey has been added to the NCOD community engagement plan at the recommendation of the HPAB in order to reach as many interested community members as possible. You are welcome to use the survey whether or not you participated in the public events. You can add any additional thoughts you may have or jump ahead to the last few questions related to visual preferences.

**[Take the NCOD Engagement Survey](https://engage.bozemanmt.gov/ncodupdate)**

The survey will be open through **June 30, 2026**.

## What's next

- Neighborhood focus group meets in late June/early July
- HPAB meeting on **July 15, 2026**

Until then, please share the survey broadly and tune in for updates on the Local Landmark Project.

For more information about the NCOD Design Guidelines Update, visit [engage.bozemanmt.gov/ncodupdate](https://engage.bozemanmt.gov/ncodupdate).
