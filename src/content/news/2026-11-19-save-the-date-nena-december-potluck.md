Come join your Northeast Neighborhood community for our bi-monthly potluck on **Tuesday, December 8th** from **6-8pm** at **Portal Thermaculture** (**660 N. Ida Ave.**).

We will have burgers and fixings to share. Bring a dish to pass if you are able, and bring your own plate and utensils if you can.

Eva Killenberg will provide information about our new Northeast Neighborhood tool library on North Church Ave, between Peach and Fridley, where neighbors can borrow tools for free. Neighbors can also join resource sharing groups and skill-sharing networks by filling out the [Google Form](https://forms.gle/GTMAZq8vu2gx9pib8).

If you have questions, contact us at [nenabozeman@gmail.com](mailto:nenabozeman@gmail.com?subject=2025%20Parade%20of%20Sheds!).
