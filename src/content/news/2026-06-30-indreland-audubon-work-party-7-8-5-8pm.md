NENA invited neighbors to a volunteer evening at the [Indreland Audubon Wetland Preserve](/places/indreland-audubon-wetland-preserve) on **Wednesday, July 8, 5:00–8:00 p.m.**, working with the Audubon Society to maintain the preserve, with **pizza and refreshments** afterward.

Please [RSVP on Partiful](https://partiful.com/e/tAJPE1fOnFRdtq5gUq8w) (or reply to the list email) so organizers have an accurate headcount for pizza.

## Parking

The preserve sits just off E. Main Street, southeast of The Village Downtown. Recommended options:

1. **Village Downtown Blvd** (recommended) — park near the mailboxes just east of N. Broadway Ave (public street), then follow the Indreland Audubon Wetland Preserve trail sign to the gate (~0.2 mile).
2. **Village Crossing Way** — public street; at the southeast end, take the gravel trail northeast to the gate (less than a block). Please do **not** park in the private Kestrel Apartments lot.
3. **Behind DA Davidson, 1101 E. Main St.** — park in the furthest northeast corner of the lot; a dirt trail leads downhill to the preserve gate.
