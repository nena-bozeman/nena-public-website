## Idaho Pole redevelopment is on the horizon

The City of Bozeman created a [TIF financing district](https://www.bozeman.net/government/economic-development/pole-yard-urban-renewal-district) for the redevelopment of this area on Nov 24, 2020. The city's vision embraces dense urban mixed use development. Is this property safe to develop? Is it appropriate for the kind of density the city envisions?

What vision do you have for this area? Please get involved to help shape the redevelopment of this area!

EPA is sponsoring a series of meetings to address the safety issues. See related news posts for meeting notes and updates.
