NENA is holding its Fall Meeting and Potluck **tomorrow (Thursday) at the Public Safety Center Community Room** (**901 N. Rouse**). The potluck starts at **5:00**, with the meeting following at **5:30 PM**. Please bring a dish to share and a plate or utensils if possible; cold seltzer waters will be provided.

**Agenda Highlights:**
* **Proposed City Charter:** Neighbor Barb Cestero will present information on the charter on the November ballot and answer questions. For details, visit [chartingbzn.com](https://www.bozemanmt.gov/services/bozeman-city-study-commission).
* **Bozeman Creek Visioning Plan:** Jim Madden will provide a slideshow preview of the plan ahead of the December City Commission presentation. Learn more at [https://engage.bozemanmt.gov/bzncreek](https://engage.bozemanmt.gov/bzncreek).

For questions or comments, reach out to NENA President Amy Hoitsma at [NENABozeman@gmail.com](mailto:NENABozeman@gmail.com). You can also connect with NENA on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nenabozeman/), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/BozemanNENA/), or via their [Website](https://www.nenabozeman.org/).
