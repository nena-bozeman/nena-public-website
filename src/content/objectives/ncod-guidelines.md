The **Neighborhood Conservation Overlay District (NCOD)** is a city zoning overlay created in 1991 to conserve neighborhood character and protect historic properties around Bozeman's historic core. The NCOD was created to stimulate the restoration and rehabilitation of structures while also inviting new construction that enhances the character and function of the area. The Bozeman Guidelines for Historic Preservation and the Neighborhood Conservation Overlay District (NCOD) were originally adopted in 2006 and has a minor update in 2015. 

Bozeman has hired a consultant team to help update the **NCOD design guidelines** and related historic preservation policies with consultant-led outreach. This project aims to update the design guidelines to consider current best practices in historic preservation, respond to community goals and values, and support the ongoing Local Landmark Project.

NENA tracks public meetings, neighborhood walks, and **Inter-Neighborhood Council** votes that affect the NCOD. Related news appears below; older posts document the 2018 review round.

#### **PAST NENA PROJECTS RELATED TO PRESERVING NEIGHBORHOOD CHARACTER**

*NENA has always worked to preserve the unique character and diversity that has defined the Northeasst Neighborhood. The following is a partial list of past projects that might shine light on the NCOD guidelines update.* 

**April 2017 R/UDAT:** R/UDAT (Regional/Urban Design Assistance Team) is a community design program run by AIA Communities by Design, which is part of the American Institute of Architects (AIA). The program brings together volunteer professionals to help towns and neighborhoods create actionable, visionary development plans through multi-day workshops. Bozeman held a R/UDAT in April 2017 to help neighbors envision the best possible future for the rapidly developing Northeast Neighborhood. The report can be found at: <https://static1.squarespace.com/static/52ea704be4b0119451fe9480/t/5e0b7aa9014e1a25d2b595da/1577810618885/Bozeman+FINAL+Report+edited-sm.pdf>

**August 2019 PhotoVoices NE Installation at Tinworks Art:** Using a participatory photography framework, Photovoices is a tool that empowers community participants to bring local knowledge and insights into decision-making processes resulting in better and more sustainable decisions. NENA asked neighbors to submit an image showing what they love about the NE Neighborhood, along with a short description. These were posted on the wall and visitors were invited to share their own thoughts by writing on the wall with sharpie markers.
