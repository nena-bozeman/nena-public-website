## Elections and leadership

NENA is led by volunteer officers elected by neighborhood members. Details below are carried forward from the prior nenabozeman.org elections page; the [NENA Bylaws 2024 (PDF)](/documents/migrated/nenabozeman-org/NENA%20BYLAWS%202024.pdf) is authoritative if anything differs.

### Election schedule

Officer terms are staggered so not all four offices are up in the same year. The prior site described it this way: in one cycle (in even-numbered years) members elect a **President** and **Vice President**; in the other two-year cycle they elect a **Secretary** and **Treasurer**. See the [bylaws](/documents/migrated/nenabozeman-org/NENA%20BYLAWS%202024.pdf) for the exact terms and procedures.

### Officer roles (from the bylaws)

Excerpt from **Article VI: Leadership structure** in the bylaws (see PDF for full text and cross-references such as Article VIII).

1. The NENA's leadership structure consists of four elected officers. The "regular" work of the NENA is carried out by the elected officers—President, Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer. "Regular" work includes, but is not limited to, planning and organizing bi-annual meetings, meeting with the City and other parties as appropriate, coordinating with neighborhood committees, and coordinating the development, publication, and distribution of a neighborhood newsletter. The President, Vice President, or designated representative shall attend monthly Inter-Neighborhood Council (INC) meetings on behalf of the NENA.
2. The President is the primary contact for the NENA. If the President is unavailable, the Vice President is the primary contact, then the Secretary, then the Treasurer. All officers are subject to Article VIII, section 5 before making decisions on behalf of the NENA.
3. The Secretary is required to attend both regularly scheduled NENA meetings and special, topical NENA meetings, and is the sole person responsible for recording and making the meeting minutes available as a public record. The Secretary may request help completing these tasks.
4. The Treasurer shall provide a report on the NENA's financial status at the regularly scheduled fall and spring NENA meetings.

### NENA financials

The NENA President and Treasurer are authorized to sign for the NENA checking account.
