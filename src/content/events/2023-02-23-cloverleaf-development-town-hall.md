Outlaw Partners presented information related to their concept plans for the 2-acre lot owned by Bronken's Distributing during this NENA Town Hall.

The proposal calls for 46 townhomes and 64 apartments (110 units total). The developers received approval for $2.2 million in TIF funds for infrastructure, with commitments to 14 affordable units and a four-story height cap.

See the [Cloverleaf Development](/development/cloverleaf) project page for background on the site.
