Developers of the proposed Bozeman Yards North Building held this community meeting to seek input on the proposed development and on using the City's affordable housing incentives. A [July 28, 2025 NENA email](https://mailchi.mp/8c6c7526a8a7/nena-cloverleaf-zma-vote-12849785) described the North Building as the second of two 5-story buildings at 622 East Tamarack Street, in front of the neighborhood's historic railroad depot.

The notice quotes the City's Affordable Housing Ordinance: an applicant for an affordable housing development proposing to use type A, B, or C incentives must hold a community meeting to inform residents and property owners of the proposed development and to solicit feedback from the community.

The notice said written comments would be accepted through August 14, 2025, by email to [Rob Pertzborn](mailto:rpertzborn@intrinsikarchitecture.com) or by mail to Intrinsik Architecture, 106 East Babcock Street Suite 1A, Bozeman, MT 59715.

See the [Bozeman Yards](/development/bozeman-yards) project page for background on the site.
