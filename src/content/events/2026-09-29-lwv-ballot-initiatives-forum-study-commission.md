The **League of Women Voters** hosts a **Ballot Initiatives Forum** so neighbors can learn about the ballot and the options available from the **City of Bozeman Study Commission**.

**When:** Tuesday, September 29, 6:30–7:45 p.m.

**Where:** Bozeman Public Safety Center, 901 N Rouse Ave

The forum is free of charge, and parking is free. Audience questions are welcome. Arrive early for best seating.
