The **Bozeman Local Government Study Commission** invites the public to a hearing on draft amendments to the city charter. Recommended changes are preliminary; final decisions in July will go to voters on the **November 3** ballot.

**When:** Thursday, June 18 — pizza at **5:00 p.m.**, public comment **5:30–7:30 p.m.**

**Where:** Bozeman City Hall, 121 N Rouse Ave

Join in person or watch online starting at 5:00 p.m. Submit written comments by **noon on June 18** to [govreview@bozemanmt.gov](mailto:govreview@bozemanmt.gov).

Review the [draft amended Charter and Study Commission 2024–26 Report](http://www.bozemanstudy.com) before the hearing.
