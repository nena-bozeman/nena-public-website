NENA’s annual **[Burdock Blitz](/our-work/burdock-blitz)** — help cut weed stalks that trap small birds and bats, then gather at Roly Poly for a treat.

**When:** Saturday, August 22, 9:00 a.m.–noon

**Where:** Patio between [Fink’s](/places/finks-deli) and [Biome](/places/biome-slow-craft)

Bring pruning clippers or loppers if you have them. Wear gloves, long sleeves, and long pants of fabrics that burs won’t stick to.

Afterward, [Roly Poly](/places/roly-poly-coffee-co) is donating a hot or cold coffee drink, and NENA is providing a tasty bite.

Please RSVP to [delmue@yahoo.com](mailto:delmue@yahoo.com) so we know how many to expect.

See the [August 17 announcement](/news/2026-08-17-help-our-birds-bats-and-enjoy-a-treat-after) for background on why this timing works.

See the [Mailchimp announcement](https://mailchi.mp/30e8234eb9d4/nena-cloverleaf-zma-vote-12854961) for the original invite.
