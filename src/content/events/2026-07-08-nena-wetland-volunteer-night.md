Join your neighbors as we volunteer on the [Indreland Audubon Wetland Preserve](/places/indreland-audubon-wetland-preserve)! After working with the Audubon Society to maintain this preserve, we'll also have pizza and refreshments for the volunteers.

**When:** Wednesday, July 8, 5:00–8:00 p.m.

**Where:** [Indreland Audubon Wetland Preserve](/places/indreland-audubon-wetland-preserve) — RSVP on Partiful for parking and meeting details.

Please [RSVP on Partiful](https://partiful.com/e/tAJPE1fOnFRdtq5gUq8w) so we have an accurate headcount.

**Parking (recommended first):** Village Downtown Blvd near the mailboxes just east of N. Broadway Ave, then follow the preserve trail sign to the gate (~0.2 mile). See the [June 30 invite](/news/2026-06-30-indreland-audubon-work-party-7-8-5-8pm) for alternate options — please do not park in the private Kestrel Apartments lot.
