Bozeman Creek Vision Plan Open House
Monday, November 9, 2026
4:30–7:30 pm at Bozeman City Hall

Hello neighbors --

Many of you have participated in previous community events and stakeholder meetings to help shape the future of Bozeman Creek. You are invited to drop in at an Open House to see how this input has influenced the project and preview the Draft Bozeman Creek Vision Plan.

Monday, November 9, 2026
Bozeman City Hall
4:30pm to 7:30pm

The City, SeeBozemanCreek, and the design team will share how the plan’s four focus areas came together: flood mitigation strategies, new downtown creekside parks, better connectivity, and future policy ideas. They will be available to talk through the plan, answer questions, and share the vision through visual and interactive media.
