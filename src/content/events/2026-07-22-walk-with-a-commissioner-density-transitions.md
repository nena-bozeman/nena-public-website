Join a **Walk with a Commissioner** to see high-density to low-density transitions in the Northeast Neighborhood, learn more about the current code for transitions, and share feedback on solutions that work for everyone.

**When:** Wednesday, July 22, 6:00 p.m.

**Where:** Meet at [Beall Park](/places/beall-park), near the pavilion.

Expect about **eight blocks** on the tour, plus a similar walk back to the start. All are welcome.

Also offered [Monday, July 27](/events/2026-07-27-walk-with-a-commissioner-density-transitions) at the same time and place.
