**Bridger View Neighborhood** is an 8-acre pocket neighborhood at Bridger Drive and Story Mill Road, on part of the same former trailer-park land as [Story Mill Community Park](/development/story-mill-community-park). It is outside NENA, east of the neighborhood.

[Headwaters Community Housing Trust](https://headwatershousing.org/our-work/bridger-view/) describes 62 energy-efficient homes, shared green space, a common house, and paths toward the park and downtown. It says half of the homes are priced below market. The [Landscape Architecture Foundation case study](https://www.landscapeperformance.org/case-study-briefs/story-mill) of the adjacent park says Headwaters sold 31 of the 62 homes below market, and that the neighborhood was completed in 2023. [Trust for Public Land](https://www.tpl.org/stories/story-mill-park-bozeman-montana) wrote in November 2021 that crews had broken ground that April on the 62-unit development, on eight acres at the edge of the park.

The [Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/article/2022/what-works-in-housing-affordability-creating-middle-income-housing-with-the-bridger-view-neighborhood) describes the ownership model. A buyer owns the house and leases the land from the trust. If an owner sells, the resale price is the original price plus 2.5 percent for each year of ownership, so a later buyer in a similar income range can still afford the home. The article was published in September 2022, while the homes were under construction. It said Bridger View had received Montana's first LEED Gold certification from the U.S. Green Building Council, and it expected the neighborhood to be finished in early summer 2023. This page uses the Landscape Architecture Foundation's later statement that the project was completed in 2023, and it does not give a completion month.

The earlier displacement of the trailer park is on the [Story Mill Community Park](/development/story-mill-community-park) page.

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

These are the homes that came back to land where trailer-park residents had been displaced. Thirty-one of them were sold below market, and the land trust sets the price when they sell again. The neighborhood shares an edge with Story Mill Community Park, which northeast neighbors reach by trail. NENA's [affordable housing](/our-work/affordable-housing) page is the association's longer note on housing cost.
