The **Rouse Avenue reconstruction** is a Montana Department of Transportation rebuild of Rouse Avenue, Montana Highway 86, from Story Mill Road to Main Street.

Rouse Avenue runs through the Northeast Neighborhood. The project also continues south of Mendenhall Street to Main Street and north of East Oak Street to Story Mill Road, so part of it is outside NENA. Mendenhall is the south boundary and East Oak is the north boundary.

[America's Transportation Awards](https://americastransportationawards.org/montana-department-of-transportation-rouse-avenue-story-mill-road-to-main-street/) describes a $13 million project that widened the avenue and added vehicle turn lanes, more lane capacity, continuous bike lanes, sidewalk upgrades, ADA-compliant ramps, and utility upgrades. [Missouri River Contractors](https://www.missouririvercontractors.com/project/rouse-avenue) lists the job as completed. That page describes a full reconstruction of one mile of road, with new utilities, a drainage system, and box culverts.

This page does not give a completion month. A [NENA post on October 9, 2020](/news/2020-10-09-trails-projects) said the Rouse project was then complete in the area that had delayed the Front Street pathway.

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

Rouse is the continuous north-south street through the neighborhood. The rebuild is the sidewalks, bike lanes, and turn lanes people use between downtown, the neighborhood, and Story Mill. NENA's [traffic calming](/our-work/traffic-calming) notes include crossings on North Rouse. The avenue also connects to [Creekside Park](/places/creekside-park) and the [Front Street pathway](/news/2021-12-10-front-street-connector-trail).
