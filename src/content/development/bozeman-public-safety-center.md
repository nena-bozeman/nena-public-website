The **Bozeman Public Safety Center** is the city's police, municipal court, and fire campus on Rouse Avenue north of East Oak Street. East Oak is NENA's north boundary, so the campus is outside the neighborhood.

[Anderson Mason Dale Architects](https://www.amdarchitects.com/public-safety-center/) date the project to 2022. They describe 91,000 square feet on 8.6 acres. The architects say the building moved police and municipal court out of a remodeled mid-century high school onto a brownfield, and that the fire department shares the campus. They list a community room among the shared spaces.

NENA has held membership meetings in that community room, including the [October 22, 2026 fall meeting](/our-work/nena-meetings/2026-fall-october).

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

The campus is the police, court, and fire station just north of the neighborhood, on the Rouse corridor neighbors drive and walk. The community room is also a room NENA has used for potlucks and meetings.
