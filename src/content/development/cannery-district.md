**Cannery District** is a commercial district on the north side of East Oak Street at Rouse Avenue, just north of NENA. East Oak is the neighborhood's north boundary, so the district is outside the neighborhood. [NBC Montana](https://nbcmontana.com/news/local/bozeman-commissioners-vote-to-annex-cannery-district) described it as the corner of Oak and Rouse. The [Cannery District history page](https://www.cannerydistrict.com/history) lists 113 East Oak Street.

That history page says redevelopment started in 2005, to reuse the old granary and pea-canning buildings for businesses, shops, and restaurants. In 2014 the work moved into the original cannery core: the canning building, the office complex, the canning warehouse, and the powerhouse. The page says those buildings were more than 100 years old and were modernized with the industrial look kept in mind.

On July 20, 2015, the City Commission voted 5-0 to annex the district. The same night the commission voted 5-0 to rezone most of it from light manufacturing to community business. NBC Montana reported that the businesses asked to be annexed because they already had city water and sewer but were not officially inside the city, and so were outside city fire and police service. Commissioner Chris Mehl told the station that water and sewer had reached the cannery around World War I.

The district's own history describes the buildings as a commercial, retail, and residential place still in use.

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

This is the business block directly across the neighborhood's north line. Neighbors walk there for shops and services. The 2015 votes are what brought that corner into the city for fire and police protection. A [2020 NENA post](/news/2020-10-09-trails-projects) on the Front Street pathway also described a trail link toward Oak Street and the Cannery District.
