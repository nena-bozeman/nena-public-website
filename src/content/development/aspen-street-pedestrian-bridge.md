The **Aspen Street pedestrian bridge** crosses Bozeman Creek at East Aspen Street, inside the Northeast Neighborhood. The [place page](/places/aspen-street-bridge) describes the link between the Bozeman Creek trail and East Aspen Street, on the public right-of-way between Rouse Avenue and the Church Street Trail. That page says the City Commission approved the project in 2011, and that the Northeast Urban Renewal Board later selected it for neighborhood trail access.

A [November 30, 2021 NENA post](/news/2021-11-30-aspen-street-bridge) says the board had selected construction, with KLJ engineering the bridge and neighborhood architect Ken VanDeWalle contributing the design. The post said the goal at that time was to finish before the end of the 2022 construction season. An [April 2021 update](/news/2021-04-16-aspen-st-bridge-update) described budget trade-offs between the bridge and street work along Front Street. This page does not give an opening date. The bridge is open.

NENA's notes on the district that selected the work are on the [Northeast Urban Renewal District](/our-work/northeast-urban-renewal-district) page. A short companion is on [Development Watch](/development/northeast-urban-renewal-district). The [Bozeman Creek Vision Plan](/our-work/bozeman-creek-vision-plan) is the city's later planning for the creek corridor.

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

The bridge is a walking connection across the creek in the middle of the neighborhood, between the streets and the trail. NENA followed the funding for years because the same urban-renewal budget was also paying for street projects.
