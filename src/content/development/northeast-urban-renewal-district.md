The **Northeast Urban Renewal District** is the city's tax-increment district in northeast Bozeman. This page is background. NENA's current notes are on the [Northeast Urban Renewal District](/our-work/northeast-urban-renewal-district) page, and this page does not repeat that material.

The Our Work page says the district uses tax increment financing to fund public improvements, and that projects have included Peach and Tamarack street improvements and the [Aspen Street pedestrian bridge](/development/aspen-street-pedestrian-bridge). The City of Bozeman describes the financing tool on its [tax increment financing](https://www.bozeman.net/departments/economic-development/tax-increment-financing) page.

The district includes work in and next to the neighborhood. This page does not restate a boundary, an ordinance number, or an adoption date.

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

Tax-increment funds from this district have paid for projects neighbors use, including the Aspen Street bridge and street work on Peach and Tamarack. When the Northeast Urban Renewal Board sets a work plan, NENA has commented on the pieces that change trails and streets. Follow that on the [Our Work page](/our-work/northeast-urban-renewal-district).
