The **AIA R/UDAT Northeast Neighborhood Plan** is a 2017 community design study of the Northeast Neighborhood. R/UDAT is the American Institute of Architects' Regional/Urban Design Assistance Team, a volunteer visit. The plan is a set of recommendations. It is not a city ordinance.

The study is about this neighborhood. It also looks at edges beyond NENA's boundary, including the Rouse corridor and the [Idaho Pole](/development/idaho-pole) site.

The [R/UDAT report](https://communityhub.aia.org/HigherLogic/System/DownloadDocumentFile.ashx?DocumentFileKey=8571fed4-1b66-8262-1deb-3305da1ccd3d) says team leader Terry Ammons completed an initial visit on February 6, 2017, and that the community process ran April 7–10, 2017. The report says a public workshop drew over 150 participants. [Studio Ammons](http://www.studioammons.com/aiardat-bozeman-northeast-neighborhood-plan) says the process brought out over 400 citizens at the community design meetings, and that residents said they wanted to "keep Northeast funky" and keep the neighborhood's character and scale. Studio Ammons describes the plan as an arts and culture district, a retail core, and parks.

The report's contents sort the recommendations into four districts: the Core, Transitional, the Gateway, and the Pole Yard.

[Topos & Anthros](https://www.toposandanthros.com/blog/2017/4/11/a-vision-for-bozemans-northeast-neighborhood) published a neighborhood account of the visit on April 11, 2017.

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

This is the design visit neighbors hosted for the northeast side, and the writeup people still use when they talk about the Core, the Rouse gateway, and the Pole Yard. [VisionNE](/our-work/visionne) is NENA's later work on neighborhood character and planning. The report is a reference for that conversation. It is not adopted city code.
