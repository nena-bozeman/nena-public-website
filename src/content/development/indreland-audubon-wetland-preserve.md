The **Indreland Audubon Wetland Preserve** is a wetland east of Village Downtown, along East Main, outside NENA. NENA's east boundary is North Broadway, Front Street, and the railroad.

The [Bozeman Wetlands history](https://bozemanwetlands.org/WP/history/) says Ileana Indreland and Michael Delaney gifted 32.4 acres to Sacajawea Audubon Society on December 28, 2018. The same page says about seven more adjacent acres were in the process of being purchased, and that the combined site has been designated the Indreland Audubon Wetland Preserve. Ileana Indreland is quoted there: "Taking care of the land has always been a big part of my family heritage. What better way to leave a legacy for the future than to put this key property in the capable hands of Sacajawea Audubon Society for all to enjoy."

The [place page](/places/indreland-audubon-wetland-preserve) describes the preserve along the East Main corridor, with views from the Milwaukee Railroad Berm Trail.

NENA has invited neighbors to volunteer there, including a [June 30, 2026 work party](/news/2026-06-30-indreland-audubon-work-party-7-8-5-8pm) and the [July 8, 2026 evening](/news/2026-07-08-tonight-indreland-audubon-volunteer-pizza).

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

The preserve is outside the boundary, and it is a place neighbors already show up to work. The 2018 gift put these acres in Audubon stewardship beside the east side of town.
