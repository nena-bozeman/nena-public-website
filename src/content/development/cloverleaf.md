**Cloverleaf** is a mixed-use residential development by [Outlaw Real Estate Partners](https://www.outlawrealestatepartners.com/cloverleaf/) on the 2-acre former Bronken's Distributing site at the corner of Peach and Plum streets, adjacent to the Wildlands project in northeast Bozeman.

Outlaw Partners proposes 46 townhomes and 64 apartments for a total of 110 units. The project received approval for $2.2 million in TIF funds to help build infrastructure, in exchange for a commitment to 14 affordable units and a height restriction of four stories.

On February 23, 2023, Outlaw Partners presented their concept plans at a [NENA Town Hall](/events/2023-02-23-cloverleaf-development-town-hall).
