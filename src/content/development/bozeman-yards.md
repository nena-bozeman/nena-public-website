**Bozeman Yards** is a mixed-use development in the Northeast Neighborhood, in front of the historic railroad depot.

A [July 28, 2025 NENA email](https://mailchi.mp/8c6c7526a8a7/nena-cloverleaf-zma-vote-12849785) describes two 5-story buildings at 622 East Tamarack Street. The proposed North Building is the second of the two. The notice quotes the City's Affordable Housing Ordinance: an applicant for an affordable housing development proposing to use type A, B, or C incentives must hold a community meeting to inform residents and property owners of the proposed development and to solicit feedback from the community.

On July 31, 2025, developers of the proposed North Building held a [community meeting](/events/2025-07-31-bozeman-yards-north-building-community-meeting) at Intrinsik Architecture to seek input on the proposed development and on using the City's affordable housing incentives.

On May 5, 2026, Bozeman Real Estate Group published [Bozeman Yards, a mixed-use development in northeast Bozeman](https://bozemanrealestate.group/blog/bozeman-yards-project). That article calls Bozeman Yards a 5-story mixed-use condo project across from the old Northern Pacific Depot, spanning two city blocks, with three residential buildings and 39 units ranging from one to four bedrooms. It says the project broke ground in May 2026 and that condo residences are listed at 805 N Ida Avenue. It places the property in the [Neighborhood Conservation Overlay District](/our-work/ncod-guidelines), outside the Historic District, within walking distance of Story Mill Park, nearby trails, and downtown. The article also describes marketed features: large windows, terraces, Bridger Mountain views, reserved parking, private storage, landscaped outdoor areas, and a private amenity space called The Yards Club with a fitness studio, sauna, cold plunge, plunge pool, and owner's lounge.

The 2025 email counts two buildings at 622 East Tamarack Street. The 2026 article counts three residential buildings and lists condos at 805 N Ida Avenue.
