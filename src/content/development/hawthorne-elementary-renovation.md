The **Hawthorne Elementary renovation and addition** is the school project at 405 East Mendenhall Street. Hawthorne is inside the Northeast Neighborhood, on the south edge. Mendenhall Street is the neighborhood's south boundary. The same block is the subject of the history entries for the [1883 East Side School](/history/1883-hawthorne-elementary-east-side-school-opens) and the [1939 rebuild](/history/1939-hawthorne-elementary-school-rebuilt).

[NBC Montana](https://nbcmontana.com/news/local/bozeman-school-board-approves-final-designs-for-school-renovations_2016051215152120) reported that on March 28, 2016, the Bozeman Public Schools Board of Trustees unanimously approved final designs for renovating and expanding Hawthorne, and designs for Sacajawea Middle School. For Hawthorne, the article says the cafeteria would move from the basement to the main floor, existing classrooms would be renovated, and a 12,000-square-foot two-story addition would hold at least seven classrooms and a special education room, including space for classes that had been in modular units. It also describes a new front office where staff could control who came in. The article says the Hawthorne project would cost around $5 million over a 16-year period. It says voters had approved bonds for the school projects the year before.

Sten Witmer of Comma-Q Architecture told NBC Montana that the Hawthorne drawings were wrapping up, the project would go out for bid on April 13, construction would start in June 2016 when school let out, and the work would wrap up in summer 2017. That schedule is what the architect said when the board approved the designs.

## Why it matters to the neighborhood

Hawthorne is the elementary school on the neighborhood's south edge, beside Beall Park. The plan the board approved moved the cafeteria out of the basement and replaced modular classrooms with rooms in the addition. Families on this side of town use the building every school day.
